Safari Bookmark Sync V0.9

Public preview __VERSION__. Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13 or later.

This installer places the app in:

/Applications/Safari 收藏夹同步.app

  • Choose Edge → Safari or Safari → Edge.
  • Incremental and mirror modes in both directions.
  • Complete backups of both browsers before each sync.
  • English and Simplified Chinese, with a menu bar Quick Panel.

Quit the previous version before installation. Installing does not synchronize bookmarks or change existing backups. This local preview installer is not Developer ID signed or notarized.
