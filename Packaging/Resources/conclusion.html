Ready to Try V0.9

  1. Open Safari Bookmark Sync from Applications.
  2. In Settings, choose the bookmark files and check access. If denied, use Open Full Disk Access Settings and add the installed app.
  3. Choose the sync direction and mode. Back up first, then verify the first sync result.

Closing the main window leaves the menu bar Quick Panel available. If Bartender hides its icon, place this app in Show, or use the Dock menu → Open Quick Panel.

Settings → General → Language offers Follow System, English, and Simplified Chinese. Reopen the app to apply a language change.

V0.9 is a preview. No bookmarks or backups are uploaded by the app.
